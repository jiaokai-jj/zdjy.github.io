权眸 · 隐私政策

适用于「权眸」与「权眸Pro」两款产品　|　最后更新：2026 年 10 月 1 日
一句话总结：权眸不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息。全部数据处理都在您自己的电脑上完成。

一、我们收集哪些信息

不收集。权眸在运行时不会采集您的姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、位置、照片、银行卡或任何可用于识别个人身份的数据。

二、数据存放在哪里

软件产生的全部数据都保存在本机，包括：

  • 软件配置与用户自定义规则
  • 操作日志（供您自行复盘）
  • 授权文件

这些数据存放在您电脑的用户目录与应用程序数据目录中，不会离开您的设备，卸载软件时可选择一并删除。

三、软件何时联网

仅在授权验证这一个场景联网，且只在您主动激活或软件定期校验授权状态时发生：

传输内容设备机器码、授权码
用途验证授权是否有效、是否被吊销
不传输的内容持仓、账户、成交、日志、任何个人身份信息
服务地址https://www.jyt.cc.cd

除授权验证外，软件不发起任何网络连接；行情与交易数据来自您本机已安装的交易终端，不经过我们的服务器。

四、第三方

  • 未集成任何广告 SDK
  • 未集成任何统计分析或用户行为追踪组件
  • 不与任何第三方共享数据（因为我们没有数据可共享）

五、权限说明

软件以普通用户权限运行，不需要管理员权限。它仅读取您本机交易终端窗口中的内容（在您明确操作后），不修改系统设置、不修改其他应用程序、不创建自启动项或后台服务。

六、儿童隐私

本软件面向已开通证券交易权限的成年投资者，不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

七、您的权利

由于我们不持有您的任何数据，您无需向我们提出查询、更正或删除请求 —— 相关数据全部由您本机的软件自行管理，您可随时查看或删除。

Privacy Policy (English)

Applies to "Quanmou" and "Quanmou Pro". Last updated: October 1, 2026.

Summary: Quanmou does not collect, upload, or share any personal information. All data processing happens locally on your own computer.

1. Information we collect

None. Quanmou does not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location, photos, bank card details, or any other personally identifiable data.

2. Where data is stored

All data the app produces is stored locally: application settings and user-defined rules, operation logs (for your own review), and license files. These reside in your user profile and application data folders, never leave your device, and can be removed when you uninstall the app.

3. When the app connects to the network

Only for license verification, and only when you activate the app or when it periodically re-checks the license status. The data sent is the device machine code and the license key. Position data, account data, trade records, logs, and any personal information are never transmitted. Outside license verification the app makes no network connections; market and trading data come from the trading terminal installed on your own computer and do not pass through our servers.

4. Third parties

No advertising SDKs, no analytics or behavioral tracking components, and no data sharing with third parties (we have no data to share).

5. Permissions

The app runs with standard user privileges and does not require administrator rights. It only reads content from the window of the trading terminal installed on your machine, after an explicit action by you. It does not change system settings, modify other applications, or create startup entries or background services.

6. Children's privacy

This software is intended for adult investors who hold a securities trading account. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for you to request, correct, or delete from us. All related data is managed by the app on your own machine and can be viewed or deleted by you at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the date above.
